# Screenshots read by `captures-ocr.test.ts`

Phone-sized captures (412×915 CSS pixels at a 2.625 device pixel ratio, as on a
Pixel), taken while scrolling, once with the overlap a reader leaves between two
screens (`*-overlap-*`) and once with screens that only touch (`*-flush-*`).

- `wiki-*`: the French Wikipedia article « Désinformation » on the mobile site,
  in light mode and in dark mode (`wiki-dark-*`), with sections opened. A status
  bar (clock and battery that change on each capture) and an app tab bar are
  drawn over the page, as a phone and a reader app add them. Text under
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), Wikipedia
  contributors.
- `yt-*`: the « Afficher la transcription » panel of the video « Manipulations
  de l'information : le récap » (Ministère de l'Europe et des Affaires
  étrangères, `73Z-p6pfMiY`), at a phone's width, YouTube's header and its
  « Synchroniser avec la vidéo » button included. Published under the Creative
  Commons Attribution licence (CC BY).
